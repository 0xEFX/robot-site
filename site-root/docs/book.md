# Written Works of Dorian Roland

## Gone Dark

<img src="../img/gd_mockup.png" alt="Architecture" style="max-width: 500px;">

A former soldier turned cyber sleuth, known only as Narrator, belongs to a secret online society dedicated to unraveling the mysteries of the Net of Lights; an AI system capable of predicting human behavior on a global scale. When the system suddenly loses track of half the population it monitors, Narrator is drawn into a covert race to uncover the cause while keeping the AI’s existence hidden from the world. As he follows a trail of deception stretching back decades, he becomes entangled between powerful corporate and military interests, unsure whom to trust. To uncover the truth, he must expose the forces behind the Net of Lights, understand its true purpose, and decide whether the knowledge it offers is worth the price of possessing it.
